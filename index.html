Hi, I am Sifar.

I live in Dhaka and I like making things for the web that feel alive: sites that move gently, games that open in a browser tab, and little tools that make life easier for the people around me.

By day I am a Creative Associate at Kashful Foundation, a youth led organisation working on civic, digital, gender and climate rights. After hours I build side quests, like an open world game set on my own streets and a futsal app for my squad.

Off screen you will find me on a futsal turf, inside a thriller novel, or happily watching the rain.
