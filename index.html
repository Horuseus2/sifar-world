Hi, I am Sifar.

I love to create things. Websites, games, little apps, campaigns: if it can grow out of an idea and a free weekend, I probably want to try building it.

I graduated from Bangladesh University of Professionals with a Bachelor of Business Administration (General), majoring in Marketing. Marketing is the other thing I love: working out who something is for, and how to make them care about it.

I have taken that mix onto business case competition stages across the country, and into side projects that my friends actually use. Off screen you will find me on a futsal turf, inside a thriller novel, or happily wrapped up in a Dhaka winter.
