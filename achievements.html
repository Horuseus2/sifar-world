Champion · 2025

CaseSprint 2025

Jatiya Kabi Kazi Nazrul Islam University

A national business case competition organised by the JKKNIU Career Club and powered by Ghari.

In the grand finale we presented a market engagement plan for electric vehicles built on three steps: Attract, Convert, Transform. Build awareness, remove the barriers to buying, then turn owners into advocates. The judges liked it, and we lifted the champion's trophy.
